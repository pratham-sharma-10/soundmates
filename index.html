Want to get past small talk and meet people on a deeper frequency?

Soundmates is a quiet, intentional space for anyone who loves music. We gather, settle into the present moment, and listen to the soundtracks of our lives together.

How it works

  1. Share your trackBring a song that means a lot to you right now. The one you’ve been playing loud in the car, or the one keeping you grounded. Some days we keep songs to about 2 minutes.
  2. Listen togetherThe room hears each song at once, fully present.
  3. Notice how it feelsAfter each song, a short, easy conversation about what the sound did in your body and where it took you.

Skipping the usual social scripts and going straight to the songs that move us makes a real connection happen fast.

No two sessions are the same. Different people come each time, and they bring a different soundtrack. It only happens once.
